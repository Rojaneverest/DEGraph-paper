REFERENCE:
A claim qualifies as Inpatient Rehabilitation iff BOTH of the following hold (compound AND): (1) `upper(trim(type_of_bill_hdr))` matches the SQL `LIKE '11%'` pattern — i.e. the Type of Bill string starts with the two characters '11' (inpatient facility classification), tested case-insensitively after trimming; AND (2) an `exists(...)` over the `revenue_code_list` array returns true, where the lambda is `x -> x is not null AND trim(x) != '' AND lpad(regexp_replace(trim(x), '[^0-9]', ''), 4, '0') IN ('0118', '0128', '0129', '0138', '0158')`. The revenue-code test normalizes each entry by trimming, stripping non-digit characters via `regexp_replace(..., '[^0-9]', '')`, left-padding to 4 digits with `lpad(..., 4, '0')`, and then membership-testing against the literal CMS rehabilitation revenue-code set `{0118, 0128, 0129, 0138, 0158}`. The TOB-only condition is necessary but not sufficient; the revenue-code-only condition is necessary but not sufficient; only their conjunction classifies the claim as Inpatient Rehabilitation. This is in contrast to the Hospice predicate (TOB '81%' + a different revenue set {0651, 0652, 0655, 0656}) and the Home Health predicate (TOB '32%' OR '33%' alone, no revenue check).
