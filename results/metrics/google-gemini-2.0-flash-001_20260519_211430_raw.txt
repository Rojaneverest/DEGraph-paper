REFERENCE:
Two joins in the file: (1) `refunds_raw` ⨝ `returns_raw` ON `return_id` (inner join), (2) the result ⨝ `orders_raw` ON `order_id` (left join). If `return_id` becomes nullable in `refunds_raw`, the inner join on `return_id` silently drops every refund row with null return_id — silver.refunds_enriched will under-report refund volume. Because the second join is LEFT, the orders side does not introduce additional row loss, but `total_amount` becomes NULL for orphan-order refunds.
