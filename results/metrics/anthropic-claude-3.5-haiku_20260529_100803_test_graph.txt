Score: None

REFERENCE:
`cond_er_obs` is a compound AND of two parts: (A) a non-elective admit check, and (B) an ER/Observation revenue-or-TOB check. Part A: `F.coalesce(F.col('admit_status_hdr'), F.lit('')) != F.lit('1')` — i.e. admit_status is NOT '1' (elective), with NULL coalesced to '' so a missing admit_status counts as non-elective (passes A). Part B is an OR of two conditions: (B1) `exists(revenue_code_list, x -> x is not null AND trim(x) != '' AND lpad(regexp_replace(trim(x), '[^0-9]', ''), 4, '0') IN ('0762','0450'))` — any revenue code normalizing to 0762 (observation) or 0450 (emergency room); OR (B2) `upper(trim(type_of_bill_hdr)) LIKE '13%'` — Type of Bill starting with 13 (hospital outpatient). A claim is Emergency/Observation iff A AND (B1 OR B2). The revenue-code normalization (trim, strip non-digits via regexp_replace, lpad to 4 with '0') is the same pattern used by the Rehab and Hospice predicates, only with a different code set.
