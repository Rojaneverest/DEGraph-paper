Q: In `silver/shipments_timeline.py`, the file joins shipments_raw with orders_raw. What is the join key, and what columns from orders_raw propagate into the silver output?
Score: None

REFERENCE:
Join key: `order_id` (inner join). The orders side is narrowed via `.select('order_id', 'customer_id')` before the join, so only `customer_id` propagates into `silver.shipments_timeline` from `orders_raw` (in addition to the join key `order_id`). The other orders columns (quantity, total_amount, currency, etc.) do not appear in the silver output because of that projection. The aggregated shipment columns (shipment_count, first_ship_ts, last_delivered_ts, carriers_used) come from the shipments aggregate, not from orders_raw.
