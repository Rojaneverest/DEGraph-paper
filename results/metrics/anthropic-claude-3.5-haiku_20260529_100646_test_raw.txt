`DeltaMergeSink.run(df)` branches on whether the target already exists: `if not spark.catalog.tableExists(self.target_table): df.write.format("delta").saveAsTable(self.target_table); return`. On SUBSEQUENT runs (table exists) it performs a MERGE: `target = DeltaTable.forName(spark, self.target_table)`, builds the match condition by joining the merge keys (`" AND ".join(f"t.{k} = s.{k}" for k in self.merge_keys)`), then `target.alias("t").merge(df.alias("s"), condition).whenMatchedUpdateAll().whenNotMatchedInsertAll().execute()` — i.e. update matched rows in place and insert new ones (SCD-Type-1 upsert).
