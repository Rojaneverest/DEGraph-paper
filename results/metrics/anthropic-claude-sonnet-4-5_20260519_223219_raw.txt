## Affected Tables

### Silver Layer

1. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.orders_cleaned`**
   - **Reason**: Directly reads from `orders_raw` and passes through the `customer_id` column without renaming it. The column is used in the final output schema.

2. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.customer_profile`**
   - **Reason**: Reads `orders_raw` and joins it with `customers_raw` on `customer_id`. The join condition would break, and the column is used as a grouping key in the final aggregation.

3. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.refunds_enriched`**
   - **Reason**: Reads `orders_raw` and joins it with the refunds→returns chain on `order_id`. While the primary join is on `order_id`, the `customer_id` column from orders is included in the final output for downstream analyses.

4. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.shipments_timeline`**
   - **Reason**: Reads `orders_raw` specifically to pull `customer_id` into the shipment timeline via `orders_df.select("order_id", "customer_id")` and includes it in the final output.

### Gold Layer

5. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.customer_ltv`**
   - **Reason**: Reads from `customer_profile` (which would already be broken). The `customer_id` column flows through from the silver layer and is used as a key column in the output.

6. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.customer_return_rate`**
   - **Reason**: Reads from `customer_profile` and joins with `returns_cleaned` on `customer_id`. Both the join condition and the output schema depend on this column.

7. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.attribution_funnel_daily`**
   - **Reason**: Joins `orders_cleaned` with `marketing_touchpoints` using `customer_id` (via the `USING (customer_id)` clause in the SQL). The join would fail.

8. **`main.dbdemos_ecom.revenue_forecast_features`**
   - **Reason**: Reads from `customer_ltv` (which would already be broken) and uses `customer_id` as a grouping key in the final aggregation: `groupBy("customer_id", "product_id", "forecast_date")`.

## Summary

**8 tables total** would need updating:
- **4 silver tables**: orders_cleaned, customer_profile, refunds_enriched, shipments_timeline
- **4 gold tables**: customer_ltv, customer_return_rate, attribution_funnel_daily, revenue_forecast_features

The impact cascades because `customer_id` is a fundamental join key throughout the pipeline, connecting orders to customers and enabling all customer-centric analytics in the gold layer.
